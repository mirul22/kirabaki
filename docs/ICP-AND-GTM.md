# ICP and Go-to-Market

## Positioning rule

Do **not** launch as:

> “KIRABAKI is an AI finance platform for everyone.”

That is unfocused and forgettable.

Start with **one person**, then expand by ICP only after gates.

## ICP #1 — Primary (long time)

**Malaysian young / high-income professionals**

| Attribute | Detail |
| --- | --- |
| Age | ~25–40 |
| Income | Roughly RM8k–30k+ |
| Has | Salary, EPF, multiple accounts, investments, tax, insurance, family expenses, goals |
| Pain | “I’m earning decent money but I don’t actually know whether I’m financially doing well.” |
| Channel | Phone-first PWA (Chrome / Safari), Add to Home Screen |

This is the founder’s ICP. Perfect for proving the thesis.

**Message for ICP #1:**

> Tell me what is happening with my money, why it matters, what I should do, and whether I’m getting better.

## ICP #2 — Next

**Freelancer / consultant / solopreneur**

Pain: “My personal and business money are messy.”

KIRABAKI: separate them and show how healthy each side is.

Enter only after Personal alpha works for ICP #1.

## ICP #3 — Later

**Micro-SME**

Malaysia has a large microenterprise base. Pain: “I have accounting software, but I don’t understand my business.”

KIRABAKI: explain business health, receivables, cash — not another ledger table.

Enter after Personal + early Tax / Business MVP gates.

## Competitive strategy

Do **not** fight feature-for-feature on:

- Receipt OCR
- Bank connection
- AI chatbot

Those are commodities.

**Battlefield:** Can KIRABAKI understand a person’s financial life better and help them make better decisions?

## Pricing (unvalidated)

Record for orientation only. Do **not** encode in product until Gate 5 (people pay).

| Tier | Price target | Target |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Free | RM0 | Acquisition |
| Plus | RM19.90/mo | Normal user |
| Pro | RM49.90/mo | Serious user |
| Business | RM79–199/mo | SME |
| Professional | RM199–999+/mo | Accountant / tax agent |
| Enterprise | Custom | Larger organisations |

Pricing must be validated, not imagined.

## Product KPIs by stage

| Stage | Question |
| --- | --- |
| Phase 1 (you) | Does it improve *my* financial behaviour? |
| Alpha (~20) | Do they come back? |
| Beta (~100) | Do they trust recommendations? |
| Paid (~100 paying) | Will people pay? |
| Growth (~1,000) | Can we acquire efficiently? |
| Scale (10,000+) | Do infra / support economics work? |

## North star (always)

**Financial Improvement Rate** — see [THESIS.md](./THESIS.md).

## GTM discipline

1. Founder uses real data weekly (Gate 1)
2. 20 users for 30+ days (Gate 2)
3. ≥30% complete recommended actions (Gate 3)
4. People ask to keep it (Gate 4)
5. People pay (Gate 5)
6. Referrals (Gate 6)
7. Businesses pay (Gate 7)

No heavy hiring, ads, or complex infrastructure before Gate 5.
