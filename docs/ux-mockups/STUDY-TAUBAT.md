# Study: Taubat.app + Izuddin Helmi

Reference only. Do **not** copy Taubat’s green, gold, crescent, Playfair lockup, or Islamic motif. Borrow the *craft*, not the costume.

Sources: [taubat.app](https://www.taubat.app/), [taubat.app/brand](https://www.taubat.app/brand), [dribbble.com/izuddinhelmi](https://dribbble.com/izuddinhelmi).

---

## Who he is

Izuddin Helmi (Malaysia) — mobile / visual / UX. Two public hands:

1. **Taubat** — spiritual companion. Cream marketing, deep-green product, no-shame copy.
2. **Jupiter / Mercurial** (Dribbble) — dark, precise trading UI. Dense but aligned. Almost no decoration.

The common thread is not “Islamic green” or “crypto dark.” It is **restraint**: every type role is assigned, one accent, space or alignment doing the work, no mascot, no gradient soup.

Hero: *It’s never too late **to return.*** — serif, italic on the verb. Subcopy is a companion, not a coach.

Brand page: cream header over a deep green field. Metadata in mono. Feels like a printed document, not a SaaS template.

## What “clean” means in his work

1. **Fewer typefaces, stricter jobs.** Serif = emotion. Sans = use. Mono = spec.
2. **One accent.** Gold is rare. Green is structure. Nothing else shouts.
3. **Air.** Headlines are large; sections are not stacked cards.
4. **Dark is a material**, not a theme toggle. Marketing is cream; the instrument is dark.
5. **No persona blob.** The mark is a path. Icons are one stroke.
6. **Copy is designed.** Short, concrete, never “Let’s do it!!”
7. **Jupiter side:** 1px borders, alignment, components that look machined. Clean ≠ empty.

## What KIRABAKI should steal (method)

- Cream page + warm ink + **one** copper accent (not their gold, not their green)
- **Newsreader** (not Playfair) for greetings / Next Move titles; **Inter** for UI; **JetBrains Mono** for kickers and amounts
- Next Move as a **dark cocoa card** (instrument), not a teal marketing block
- Path as a single stroke + one node — no fat candy trail
- Floating dark tab bar (from the Taubat *app*, not the landing)
- Italic on one feeling word (`stronger`)
- No Kira face, no two-square logo toy

## What we must not steal

Playfair lockup, forest/sage/gold stack, crescent-star, Islamic geometric wash, “Taubat” voice, Jupiter’s crypto chrome.
